Privacy

cube.nathanwei.org · Updated October 15, 2026

What is stored

When you sign in with Google, this site stores your Google account's name, email address and profile picture, and the solves you record: scrambles, times, the moves of each solve, and the analysis computed from them.

Where

In a database on Cloudflare, which hosts the site. A cookie keeps you signed in. Cloudflare processes requests to the site under its own privacy policy.

What it is used for

Only to show you your own solves, statistics and training. There are no ads. Your data is not sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Email nwei@college.harvard.edu and your account and solves will be deleted.
